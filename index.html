ABOUT

I am a PhD student in the Psycholinguistic and Cognitive Modelling Lab at the University of Stuttgart, advised by Titus von der Malsburg. My research broadly focuses on real-time sentence processing.

Currently, as part of a DFG-funded project, I primarily use eye-tracking techniques to investigate the incremental processing of resumptive pronouns in German.

Prior to joining Stuttgart, I completed my Master's degree in Cultural and Cognitive Linguistics at the University of Munich (LMU).
